Who belongs to the tenant, the groups they are in, the roles there are, and who is bound to what.

## Long lists

A list of members loads fifty at a time. Search by email or name asks the server, so it finds a member however far down the list they are, and **Load more** brings the next fifty. The same holds for tenants, instance members, the pickers that add people to a group, and events. A list that has not yet been given paging says "Showing the first 200" and does not pretend to be whole.

## Going back to an access release

**Go back to this** on an earlier access release first lists what it would remove, under "These are removed". When anything is removed, the confirmation is a danger confirmation. Nothing is done until you confirm.
