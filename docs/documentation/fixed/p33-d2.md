Choices to know of:
- An entry nothing dates (`CHANGELOG.md` has no dates, and none is made up) comes first, as "not released"; the generated entries are ordered by reverse path (`unreleased`, `0-5-0`, ...).
- "A link to the next set" is a link to the newest entry not shown ("3 older entries, from X"): a static site has no paging.
- An entry's title is a heading under the page's own; its body's shallowest heading sits directly under it; ids are prefixed with the entry's path; an entry holding `::changelog` is not drawn inside itself.
- The feed's links are whole when there is an origin (the tenant route and `/docs` use the request's), paths otherwise (the export without `--origin`, as the sitemap).
- Links in `CHANGELOG.md` to files of the repository are written as their words in the generated pages (they lead nowhere in the docs).
