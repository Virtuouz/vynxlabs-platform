Guides for the tables of a workspace: finding and changing their rows, changing their columns, deciding who sees which rows, retiring what is no longer used, and keeping files.

- [Work with a table's rows](table-rows): filter, sort, change, add, delete and export rows, and save a view.
- [Change a table's columns](table-definition): add columns and set their rules, type, values, indexes and display column, and take a change back.
- [Decide who sees which rows](row-rules): set row rules, and check them as a person or a group.
- [Retire a table or a column, and bring it back](retired-data): take data out of use without losing it, bring it back, or delete it for good.
- [Store and delete files](files): attach files to rows, upload them, and see what keeps a file.
