Read [the concepts](explanation/concepts) first. They are few, and every screen is made of them.

Then:

- [Sign in to the console](how-to/basics/sign-in), and [find anything and jump to it](how-to/basics/find-and-jump) once you are in.
- [Finding your way](reference/basics/finding-your-way) names every part of the frame around each screen.
- [Your tenants](../console/reference/screens/home/tenants) is the list at the root address. When you open the console again it takes you back to the last workspace overview or tenant you were in, and the list stays one click away.
- [Your account](../console/reference/screens/account/account) holds your API keys, your name, your password and your sessions. It has no page of preferences: your settings follow you without one.
