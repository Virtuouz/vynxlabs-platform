What is running, what ran, and what changes next.

- [Runs](runs): Every run of a script or a flow: what started it, what it was given, its logs and its result.
- [Events](events): Events published in the workspace, and what each one started.
- [Triggers](triggers): What starts work without a person: schedules, webhooks, events and forms.
- [Approvals](approvals): Flows waiting for someone to say yes or no, and who may answer.
- [Releases](releases): What is deployed, the drafts waiting to be, and the way to promote a dev workspace into production.
- [Git sync](git-sync): A dev workspace and a branch of the tenant's repository, kept the same.
