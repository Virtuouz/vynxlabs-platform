## Why round 2 to 4's layout fixes did not hold (the shared causes, fixed once)

- **Two lists drew their row actions, and only one had the fix.** `ListCollection` folded its actions under the first cell on a phone and kept a wide last column; `ListScreen` (used by 11 screens: Data sources, Connections, Variables, Locations, Tenant and Instance lists) had a bare last column with no width, of the first tier, and its callers stacked their own buttons in `max-sm:flex-col` (fc, item 5). Now both draw it from `kit/rowActions.tsx` (`useRowActionColumns`). Callers of `ListScreen` with `rowActions`: Tenant (3), Instance (2), Execution (3), Data, Environments, Account. A scan (`ListsPriority.test.ts`, "no screen aligns or stacks its own row actions") fails on any caller that aligns its own buttons.
- **Tiers were chosen by the window, not by the room the table has.** A list under the navigation rail (768 px window, 638 px of room) or beside an inspector showed columns it had no room for, so the last one was cut. `DataTable` now measures its own box and shows the highest tier whose minimum fits (`fittingTier`, `tableMinimums`), and its minimum never exceeds the box (`min(var(--table-min-n), 100%)`), so no list scrolls sideways because of its columns. Before it is measured (and in tests) the window's classes decide as before. The `beside` hack stays only as that fallback.
- **A label cut to a few letters, three times (round 4 item 1, then Workers, then Connections).** `StatePill` shortened any label of more than one word and any detail. A state's words are now never cut: a label wraps at its spaces, the detail wraps under it. Callers: every `StatePill` (133).
- **A name beside things that refuse to shrink** (Overview's triggers, the App Builder bar, version rows) was left a few pixels. The name keeps a minimum and what does not fit wraps under it.
