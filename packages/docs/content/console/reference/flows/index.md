The pages here say what the flow editor asks for each step and each start, and what limits apply. The generated pages in Develop list the same parts by the names a file uses.

- [Flow steps](steps): each step in the editor, its fields, what it gives and its limits.
- [Ways a flow starts](starts): the five starts, their settings and limits.
- [Expressions in a flow](expressions): where an expression may be written, what it may read, and the traps.
