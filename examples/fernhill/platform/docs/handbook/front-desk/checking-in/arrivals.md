---
title: Arrivals
description: Greeting a patient and marking them as here.
---

Find the appointment, check the name and date of birth, and mark it as arrived.

:::steps
1. Find today's appointment.
2. Check the name and the date of birth.

   :::details{summary="When they do not match"}
   Ask for a second piece of identification, and tell the clinician before the visit.
   :::

3. Mark it as arrived.
:::
