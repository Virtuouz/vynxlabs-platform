Deploying drafts, promoting a version to production, rolling back, asking for a promotion and keeping a workspace in step with Git.

- [Deploy drafts to a dev workspace](deploy-drafts): review what is waiting, choose, deploy, or set aside.
- [Promote a version](promote-a-version): send what runs in a dev workspace to production or to another dev workspace.
- [Roll back to an earlier version](roll-back-a-version): put an earlier version in place, in dev or in production.
- [Ask for a promotion, and decide one](ask-for-a-promotion): when you may not promote, ask; when you may, decide.
- [Sync a workspace with Git](sync-a-workspace-with-git): follow a branch, push, pull, settle conflicts, and use a production workspace's release branch.
