# p34-d1: text that no longer fit after the 16 px scale

Owner: the top bar's "Search or jump to…" wrapped onto two lines in its pill.

## What changed
- `apps/console/src/Shell.tsx` (top bar): the right cluster is `whitespace-nowrap`; the search button's width is `calc(14rem * var(--vx-type-scale))` (was a fixed `w-56`, 224 px, which a 16 px label plus the Ctrl+K hint no longer fit), it may shrink (`min-w-0`), its label is `min-w-0 flex-1 truncate`, the hint `shrink-0`. The tenant name, its wire and "promotes to" show from `xl` (1280) instead of `lg`, so the workspace name and the current crumb keep their room between 1024 and 1279 (the crumb had been squeezed to nothing there).
- `apps/console/src/kit/status.tsx:236` `Tag`: `whitespace-nowrap`. "Not published", "Published v1", "Not a member", "Documentation" wrapped to two lines at 768 and 390. Call sites: every file that uses `<Tag>` (29: Screens, Apps, Members, tenant pages, the shell).
- `apps/console/src/screens/Queries.tsx:785`: the "Rows to return" field's `w-48` is now `12rem * scale` (its label wrapped at 3840).
- Test: `apps/console/src/enforce.test.ts`, "the top bar's items do not wrap at a larger type scale".
