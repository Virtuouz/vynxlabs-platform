Flows waiting for someone to say yes or no, and who may answer.

## Who may decide

The overview counts the approvals waiting for **you**, and the queue that "Decide" opens lists exactly those. Approvals waiting for someone else are counted apart, and **Waiting for others** lists them read-only: you can see what is waiting and who it waits for, and you are not offered Approve or Reject. A flow's run page says, step by step, whether you may decide.
