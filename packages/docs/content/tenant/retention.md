How long runs, logs and audit entries are kept.

## Keeping things for less time

Making a period longer saves at once. Making any of them shorter asks first, and names the kind and its old and new number of days, because what is older than the new period is deleted. Nothing is saved until you save the page, and **Discard** puts the numbers back.
