## Give an app a hostname of its own

The steps to give an app, or the portal with every app of a workspace, a hostname such as `desk.example.com` are on one page, with the DNS record, the refusals and how to turn the hostname off.

## Steps

:::steps
1. Follow [Serve an app on your own hostname](../sso/serve-an-app-on-your-own-hostname).
:::

## The result

The hostname's row in **Domains** is **On** and **Verified**, and the app's row in **Apps** lists the hostname under **Address**.

## If it does not work

The refusals, with their causes, are in the table under **If it does not work** on [Serve an app on your own hostname](../sso/serve-an-app-on-your-own-hostname). The screen's controls are in [the Domains screen](../../reference/screens/domains).
