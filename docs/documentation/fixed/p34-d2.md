# p34-d2: the help panel's entry is as smooth as its exit, and the page reflows with it

Owner, trying p34-c5: "The drawer animates, but it is very abrupt on the way in and the rest of the console screen pops to size instead of animating smoothly."

## Cause
- Docked (1440 and up), the panel was a `relative w-[380px]` column that appeared in one frame: the main area snapped 380 px narrower, and the only motion was a 24 px, faded slide inside the already-reserved column. At 768 it slid only 24 px with no scrim, so it read as a pop.

## What changed
- `apps/console/src/help/HelpDock.tsx`: docked, the panel now goes through `Docking` (the inspector's piece, `apps/console/src/kit/layout.tsx`, now exported), which grows the column's width from 0 to the panel's own over `motion-slow` and back, so the main area reflows with the panel, over the same time and curve (no timing copied). The panel keeps its 380 px, so its text never reflows. Over the work (768 to 1279, or beside an inspector) it slides the full width from its edge (`translate-x-full`, as `SideSheet` does) over a scrim that fades with `motion-slow`; a click on the scrim closes it. The scrim is not a trap: keyboard and page behind are unchanged. On a phone it slides in full width. `inert` while leaving, focus to the heading on open, back to the opener on close, Escape: unchanged. Reduced motion: `Docking` and `Appear` take no time.
- `apps/console/src/kit/layout.tsx:161`: `export` on `Docking` and a doc line.
- Call sites of the shared pieces (POLISH-013): `Docking` is used by `Inspector` (every screen with an inspector, which already gave room smoothly) and now `HelpDock`. `Appear` is used by the shell flyout, add menus and `HelpDock`; none had the snap.

## Tests
`apps/console/src/help/Help.test.tsx`: docked wrapper moves its width from 0 with `transition-[width] motion-slow`, leaves `inert`; over: full-width slide classes, scrim, inert while leaving; scrim click closes; reduced motion goes at once. Help and kit suites pass; console typecheck and biome clean.

## Looked at
Throwaway instance (port 4367, db d234) seeded with Fernhill, signed in as Maya. rAF sampling (about 16 ms) of main width, the column's width and the panel's left or translate, open and close, on App Builder, Dashboards, Runs, Tables at 1440 and 2560, light and dark: main width plus column width stays constant (main = 1152 - column) every frame, 43 frames in 700 ms, eased (first frame about 94 px, then smaller steps), no jank on the heavy screens. 768 and 390: translate 100% to 0 and back eased over 240 ms, main width constant, scrim present at 768. Keyboard: Enter opens with focus in the panel, Escape closes and focus returns to the button. Reload: the panel does not reopen. No console errors from the change.

## Left
The first frame of the ease-out moves about 94 px of main width at 1440; that is the curve, as for the inspector. The panel was not emulated under reduced motion in the browser (unit-tested).
