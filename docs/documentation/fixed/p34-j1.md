# p34-j1: the look-inspector slide tests on a slow runner

## Cause

`e2e/phase-28-look-inspector.spec.ts` (the column, field, step, page and outline lists, `measureList`, and
the keyboard move) measured the FLIP slide by frame count: at least 3 frames "between" a row's ends, the last
6 frames "still", and no step of 0.8 row between two frames. The slide itself is a fixed 180 ms Web Animation
(`packages/ui/src/useFlip.ts`), unchanged this phase. A CI runner draws a frame in 100 ms or more, so the 180 ms
motion fits 1 or 2 frames, the last 6 frames span the whole 500 ms wait (the slide itself), and a step between
two slow frames is large. Reproduced locally with CDP CPU throttling at 20x (the same failures: "the other row
slid", "Esc slid it back", "the keyboard's move slides"); 6x passed. The product was not slower: the
animation's duration and the rows' distance did not depend on the frame rate. (Not compared against a
phase 33 worktree: the code under test, `useFlip`, is the same, and the failures follow the frame rate.)

## Change (test only, e2e/phase-28-look-inspector.spec.ts)

- Frames carry their time and how many rows were playing a motion (`data-flip-moving`).
- "The end" is the last 150 ms of the recording, not the last 6 frames; the drag waits until no row has moved
  for 250 ms (up to 5 s) before it stops recording.
- A step between frames more than 30 ms apart does not count towards "never popped" (a slow draw, not a jump).
- The frames a slide must show is `3` at 60 fps and fewer when the frames of the motion itself were drawn
  slowly; where none can be shown, the page must be seen playing the motion. A row still never jumps whole,
  slides when frames allow, and settles in place.

## Tests

Whole spec file, 3 runs at 10x throttle: 20 passed each. Columns/fields tests at 20x twice, 6x: passed.
