## Open the help for a screen

By the end you have read the help for the screen you are on, searched the rest of it, and copied a link to a page.

## Before you start

- You are signed in. The help is served by the instance itself, so it opens without an internet connection and matches the version that is running.
- Your team's own pages (see below) show only the ones you may open.

## Read the help for this screen

:::steps
1. Press :kbd[F1], or :kbd[⌘/] (:kbd[Ctrl+/] elsewhere), or the question-mark button **Help for this page** in the header. The palette's **Help for this page** and the account menu's **Help for this page** do the same.
2. The panel opens at the page for the screen you are on. On a wide screen it sits beside your work. On a tablet, or beside a list that has its inspector open, it lies over the right edge. On a phone it fills the screen.
3. Press the same key again, press **Close help**, or press :kbd[Esc] while the focus is in the panel to close it.
:::

## Find another page

:::steps
1. Type in **Search the help**. Results list the pages that match, each with up to three places in it, and open at the heading. A search with no match says "Nothing in the help matches."
2. Press a result. The page opens and the search clears.
3. Use **Previous** and **Next** at the foot of a page to read on in order, the **Related** lists for pages of other kinds on the same subject, and the path above the title to go up. **Help home** goes to the first page.
4. **Back to the help for this page** returns to the page for your screen after you have gone elsewhere.
:::

## Share a page

Press **Copy a link to this help**. A notice says "A link to this help is copied." The link opens the console at the same address with the help panel on that page, so the person who opens it must sign in. **Open in documentation** shows the same page in full, in a new tab, on this instance. Where the instance names a public documentation site, **Open this page online** opens the page there; the online copy may be of a newer version.

## See your team's notes

Under **From your team** a page lists the pages of your workspace's own documentation that are about the same topic, when you may open them. The checkbox **Underline my team's terms on screens** underlines, in headings and descriptions on the console's screens, the words your team's documentation defines. It is kept as one of your settings and follows you from browser to browser.

## The result

The help panel shows the page that explains the screen, and the page's address, the help address, is copyable.

## If it does not work

| You see | Because | Do this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "There is no help for this screen yet." | The screen has no page of its own. | Press **Browse the help**. |
| "The help has no page called “{name}”." | A link names a page this version does not have. | Press **Help for this screen**, or **Browse the help**. |
| "The help could not be loaded." | The panel could not read the instance's help. | Reload the console; if it stays, tell an operator. |
