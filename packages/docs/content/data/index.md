Tables, the questions asked of them, and where the data lives.

- [Tables](tables): A table holds rows the workspace owns. Its columns have types, and who may read or write its rows is decided by roles and row policies.
- [Queries](queries): A saved question over one or more tables, written in SQL by someone allowed to, and answered within what the asker may read.
- [Data sources](data-sources): Databases outside the product that a workspace reads as if their tables were its own.
- [Dashboards](dashboards): Pages of charts, numbers and tables over queries, placed by drag and refreshed on a timer.
- [Building a dashboard](dashboard-builder): The builder for one dashboard: its widgets, their queries and the filters that narrow them all.
