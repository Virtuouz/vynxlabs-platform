# p34-i1: the record's host, and a check that says what went wrong

Cause: a tenant added `fly.volvel.dev`; the screen said to create TXT `_vx-challenge.fly.volvel.dev`; their provider appends the zone to Host, so `_vx-challenge.fly.volvel.dev.volvel.dev` was published and the check said only "No TXT record ... was found."

## What changed

- `apps/control-plane/src/kernel/apps/challenge.ts`: `zoneOf` (registrable domain; no PSL helper exists in the repo, so last two labels, three after a common two-part suffix such as co.uk or com.au); `challengeOf` now also returns `host` (relative to the zone) and `zone`; `challengeProblem` returns `{ met, problem }`. Every lookup is time-limited (5 s). On a miss it asks for at most two more names (`<full>.<zone>`, `<full>.<hostname>`), and says: found at the doubled name and what to set the host to; a record at the right name with another value (both shown); otherwise nothing found, with the host to use.
- `routes/apps.ts` (hostname check and `challenge` schema) and `routes/sso.ts` (domain check and `record` schema) use it. The check no longer builds its own sentence; both share one.
- `apps/console/src/kit/DnsRecord.tsx` (new, the one shared piece): Type TXT, Host (at most DNS providers), Full name, Value, each copyable, and the sentence "If your provider's zone is different, use the full name." Used by `screens/Domains.tsx` (replaces its `RecordLine`) and `screens/SingleSignOn.tsx` (the domain proof). These are all the places the record is shown.
- Docs: serve-an-app-on-your-own-hostname, prove-an-email-domain, how-hostnames-are-proved, reference/screens/domains. `pnpm docs:check` passes.

## Tests (seen failing first)

`apps/control-plane/src/challenge.test.ts` (right name, doubled name, wrong value, nothing, no answer; zone rule), `Portal.test.tsx` and `SingleSignOn.test.tsx` (host, full name, copy buttons, problem words drawn). `sso.test.ts` and `apps.test.ts` pass.

## Contract

`challenge` (hostnames) and `record` (SSO domains) gain `host` and `zone`. Nothing else.

## Left

The zone is a guess; there is no public suffix list. Rarer suffixes (e.g. `.pvt.k12.ma.us`) get the wrong zone, and the sentence tells the reader to use the full name.
