## Change your password and end sessions

You change your password and sign out every other place you are signed in. It takes a minute.

### Before you start

You sign in with a password. If your organization's sign-in is the only way you get in, you have no password here to change. If you forgot your password, ask an administrator for a [password reset link](../../../administer/how-to/access/add-a-member).

:::steps
1. Open the account menu, choose **Account** and find **Password**.
2. Type **Current password**, then **New password** (at least 12 characters) and **New password again**.
3. Press **Change password**.
4. Under **Sessions**, read the list: each row is a device, an address and when it signed in. The one you are using is marked **This session**.
5. To end one, press **End** on its row and confirm "End this session?". To end every other one, press **End other sessions** and confirm "End all other sessions?".
:::

### The result

After step 3 the notice says "Your password is changed. Your other sessions were signed out." After step 5 the list shows only the sessions you kept, and "Your other sessions are ended." is shown for the second choice. A session that was ended has to sign in again.

### If it does not work

- "The current password is not right.": check the current password.
- "Use at least 12 characters." and "The two do not match." are shown at the fields.
- **End other sessions** is disabled when there is no other session.

A console session lasts 7 days. A session you do not know is a reason to change your password first.
